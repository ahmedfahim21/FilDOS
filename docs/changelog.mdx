## Search that knows where, and when to say nothing

### One model for everything

- Text, pictures, audio and video are searched together with EmbeddingGemma 2,
  on your device. The old per-type models are gone; existing indexes rebuild
  automatically.
- iPhone HEIC photos are indexed (macOS and Windows).

### Hits know their place

- PDF results open at the matching page, with your words highlighted.
- Audio and video results play from the matching moment.
- Snippets highlight the words that matched.

### Better results

- A query with no real answer now returns nothing, instead of the nearest noise.
- Weaker hits fold under "Weaker matches".
- Codes and IDs search by exact words.
- Long PDFs and documents become searchable within minutes; the rest is read
  in a second pass.

### Search with a preview

- Results show beside a live preview of the selected one, opened at the page
  or moment that matched, with your words highlighted. The panel keeps one
  size from the first keystroke.

### Quick Look

- Press Space on any file to preview it in place: pictures, PDF pages,
  recordings, text, code and documents. Use the arrow keys to step through a
  folder.

### Lighter and safer indexing

- Secrets (`.env`, keys, certificates, credentials), caches and dataset folders
  are never indexed. Data files are searchable by exact words only.
- Models and caches unload when idle. Indexing slows down when the machine runs
  hot, and can be limited to set hours.
- File reading runs in its own watched process, so a malformed file can't stall
  the app.
- Settings → Indexing estimates how long indexing will take and shows an ETA.

</Update>

<Update label="0.1.0-beta" description="2026-07-10">

## FilDOS 0.1.0-beta

The first public beta — an AI-native file browser that runs entirely on your
device.

<Frame caption="The FilDOS file browser.">
  <img src="/images/home.jpg" alt="The FilDOS file browser" />
</Frame>

### Browse & manage files

- List, grid, and gallery views, with thumbnails and resizable columns.
- Create, rename, copy, move, duplicate, and delete — with undo.
- Drag files in and out of Finder / Explorer.
- Changes on disk show up instantly.
- Each folder remembers how you like to view it.

### Organize

- Colour-coded tags that stick to files as you move them.
- Jump back to what you touched recently.

### Search by meaning

- Find files by what they say, not just their name — text, code, PDFs, DOCX,
  and images.
- Search your photos with words, powered by CLIP.
- Narrow results with scope and time filters.

### Ask AI

- Chat with your files using an AI model that runs fully on your device.
- Mention files and folders, or run `/find` right in the conversation.
- Let it create, move, rename, or tidy up files — you stay in control.
- Pick from many local models, or bring your own.

### Canvas

- See how your files connect in an interactive knowledge graph.
- Scrub through time to watch your files evolve.

### Cloud drives

- Browse Google Drive and Dropbox right next to your local files.

### Private by default

- Search, chat, and everything AI run on your device — nothing leaves your
  machine unless you connect a cloud drive.
- Hide any file, folder, or file type from AI with one click.

</Update>
